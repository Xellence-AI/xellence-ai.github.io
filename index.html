06Questions owners ask

Before you decide.

How do you work with our IT team?

As partners. Your IT team stays in charge of your systems, and we bring the AI expertise, from setup to training and support. If you don't have an IT team, we take care of it all for you.

Is my business big enough?

If several people spend their days on customers, documents, or reports, yes. We size the system to your team.

What does it cost?

It depends on your team and goals. After the assessment, you get a fixed quote for equipment, setup, and support, with no per-seat fees.

Is private AI as capable as public AI?

For everyday work like writing, summarizing, and searching documents, yes. When a task needs more, non-sensitive work can go to a cloud service under your rules.

Will our data be used to train someone else's AI?

No. Your data stays on your equipment and is never shared without your permission.
